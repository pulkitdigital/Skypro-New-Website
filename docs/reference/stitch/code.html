STAGE 01 // ENTRY PREREQUISITE MANDATORY

Educational Qualification: 10+2 (Physics & Math)

Candidate must clear 10+2 with Physics and Mathematics. Non-science stream students (Commerce/Arts) can smoothly qualify via the National Institute of Open Schooling (NIOS) On-Demand examination.

Academic Minimum Pass in 10+2 / NIOS Open Schooling Recognized
02
STAGE 02 // AGE PARAMETERS DGCA CAR

Age Requirement: 17 Yrs (Training) & 18 Yrs (License Issue)

Cadets can enroll, commence ground school, and initiate flight training at 17 years. The DGCA Commercial Pilot License is formally endorsed once reaching 18 years of age.

Enrollment Window 17 Yrs (Start) / 18 Yrs (CPL) No Upper Limit for CPL
03
STAGE 03 // AEROMEDICAL CLEARANCE FIT TO FLY

DGCA Class 2 & Class 1 Medical Fitness

Initial Class 2 medicals certified by DGCA-approved doctors, followed by comprehensive Class 1 examinations at authorized IAF aeromedical centers (IAM Bangalore, AFCME New Delhi, MEC Jorhat, or empanelled hospitals).

Accredited Boards IAM / AFCME / Civil Centers Class 1 & Class 2 Valid

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to the most common regulatory and practical queries faced by aspiring cadets.

Can I become a pilot without Maths/Physics in 10+2?

Yes. If you did not take Physics or Mathematics in 10+2, you can complete these subjects through the National Institute of Open Schooling (NIOS) On-Demand examination. SkyPro guides cadets step-by-step through NIOS subject enrollment while beginning initial ground preparation.

How long does DGCA Ground School take?

A standard comprehensive batch takes between 4 to 6 months covering all 5 core subjects: Air Regulations, Aviation Meteorology, Air Navigation, Technical General, and Technical Specific, plus RTR(A) radio telephony. Fast-track modular options are also available.

What is a DGCA Computer Number and how do I get it?

The Computer Number is your permanent identification ID issued by the DGCA Central Examination Organisation (CEO) to appear for pilot license examinations. SkyPro provides 100% document scrutiny, Class 10/12 board verification filing, and eGCA submission support so your application is never rejected.

Should I fly in India or abroad (USA/South Africa)?

Both pathways have specific advantages. Training abroad provides predictable weather and rapid 8-10 month completion times; domestic flight training avoids foreign license conversion. In our free 1-on-1 counselling, we evaluate your financial schedule, timeline goals, and family constraints to recommend the optimal flight school.

What is the passing score for DGCA exams?

DGCA mandates a minimum score of 70% (70 marks out of 100) to pass each individual subject. SkyPro's students consistently average above 85% thanks to weekly timed mock quizzes and direct question bank reviews by veteran instructors.
